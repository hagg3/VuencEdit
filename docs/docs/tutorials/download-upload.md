## Download

1. On the splash screen click **Download**. The World Browser opens.
2. Pick **Current Server** or **Legacy Server** at the top.
3. Use **Featured** for curated worlds, **Browse** for the latest, or **Search** to find a world by name. Search results can be sorted by relevance, newest, oldest or quality.
4. Click a world to see its details.
5. Click **Save to File** to choose where it goes, or **Save & Open** to open it straight away.

Downloads are always `.eden` files.

## Upload

Uploads use plain HTTP and aren't encrypted. Don't upload anything private.

1. Save the world to a file first (<kbd>⇧⌘S</kbd>). Upload needs a saved file. If you're in an `.emod` session you'll be offered Save As `.eden` first.
2. Choose **Upload to Server…** from the application menu, or search for it with <kbd>⌘K</kbd>.
3. Pick the server: **Current** or **Legacy**.
4. Add a preview image. It's required. **Use map snapshot** renders the current map, or you can choose a PNG.
5. Click **Upload**. The bar shows progress, and **Cancel upload** stops it.
